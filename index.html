clipgod

clipgod is a small desktop tool that helps a single creator publish short gaming clips to their own TikTok account. The clips come from Twitch streams whose streamer has given permission for their use. Every post credits the streamer by name and links to their Twitch channel.

The tool runs only on the creator's own Windows computer. It signs in with TikTok Login Kit and uploads videos with the official TikTok Content Posting API.

How it works

  1. The creator signs in to TikTok once and grants access.
  2. The tool converts a clip to vertical 9:16 format.
  3. The tool checks the creator's TikTok posting settings, then uploads the clip as a draft or posts it with the privacy setting the creator chose.
