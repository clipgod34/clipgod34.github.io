Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Who is responsible

clipgod is operated by a private individual. Contact: hrsthlm@gmail.com

2. What data clipgod uses

When you sign in with TikTok, clipgod asks for these permissions (scopes):

  • user.info.basic: your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar. clipgod uses these to show which account it posts to.
  • video.upload: upload videos as drafts to your TikTok inbox.
  • video.publish: post videos to your TikTok profile with the privacy setting you choose.

clipgod also reads your TikTok posting settings (allowed privacy levels, comment, duet and stitch settings, maximum video length) right before each post. It does this so that each post respects those settings.

3. Where data is stored

clipgod runs only on your own computer. It has no server and no database. It stores the access and refresh tokens that TikTok issues in a local file on your computer only. It also keeps a local list of uploaded clips (file name, time, TikTok publish ID, status), so that no clip is uploaded twice. Your password never reaches clipgod. You sign in directly on TikTok's website.

4. Sharing

clipgod does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. It sends data only to TikTok, and only to do what you asked: upload your videos.

5. Retention and deletion

You can delete all stored data at any time by deleting the local token file and upload list. You can revoke clipgod's access to your TikTok account in the TikTok app settings. Tokens also expire automatically.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data under the GDPR. For questions, write to hrsthlm@gmail.com.
